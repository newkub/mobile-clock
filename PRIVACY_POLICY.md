# Privacy Policy for Wrikka Mobile

Effective date: 26 August 2026

## Overview

Wrikka Mobile is a personal customizable app that stores your tasks, notes, saved links, alarms, reminders, and settings locally on your device. Optional cloud sync uses Cloudflare D1 to back up your alarm and reminder data.

## Data we collect

- **Alarms and reminders**: synced to Cloudflare D1 under a random anonymous `userId`.
- **ElevenLabs API key**: stored only on your device, used locally to generate AI alarm sounds.
- **Usage data**: we do not use analytics or advertising SDKs.

## How we use data

- Alarm and reminder data is used solely for app functionality: notifications, widgets, and cross-device sync.

## Data sharing

We do not sell or share data with third parties.

## Security

Data in transit uses HTTPS. The app does not store sensitive personal information.

## Your rights

You can disable cloud sync at any time by turning off the sync feature in the app.

## Contact

For questions, open an issue at https://github.com/wrikka/wrikka-mobile/issues.
